
# ESG Learning Hub

**Your gateway to ESG education — curated courses, video libraries, practical tools, and academic research to build expertise at every level, from fundamentals to advanced practice.**

Whether you are new to ESG or a seasoned sustainability professional, continuous learning is essential in this rapidly evolving field. New regulations, frameworks, and best practices emerge regularly, and staying current requires access to high-quality educational resources. This hub organises the best free and open-access ESG learning resources available.

## Recommended Learning Path

For those new to ESG, we recommend the following progression:

1. **Start with fundamentals** — Understand what ESG means and why it matters
2. **Learn the frameworks** — Study the major reporting standards (GRI, ISSB, ESRS)
3. **Understand materiality** — Learn how to identify what matters most
4. **Build data skills** — Master ESG data collection and management
5. **Apply in practice** — Use tools and case studies to develop practical expertise
6. **Stay current** — Follow emerging topics and regulatory developments
