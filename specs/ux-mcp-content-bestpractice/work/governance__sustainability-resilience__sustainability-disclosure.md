
# Sustainability Disclosure Governance

**The governance of sustainability disclosure encompasses the board and management structures, processes, controls, and assurance mechanisms that ensure the quality, reliability, and completeness of ESG information reported to stakeholders.**

As sustainability reporting moves from voluntary to mandatory, the governance of this information must approach the rigour applied to financial reporting. Key elements include board oversight of sustainability reporting (ESRS 2 GOV-1), management accountability for ESG data quality, internal controls over sustainability information, materiality assessment processes (ESRS 2 IRO-1), assurance of sustainability disclosures, and integration with financial reporting processes. The convergence of sustainability and financial reporting under the IFRS Foundation umbrella signals the expectation of equivalent governance rigour.
