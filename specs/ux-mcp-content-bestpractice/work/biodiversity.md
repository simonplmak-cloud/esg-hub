# Biodiversity & Nature Hub

Biodiversity and nature considerations examine how businesses impact and depend on natural ecosystems. With the Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD) gaining momentum, companies must assess their impacts on nature, dependencies on ecosystem services, and opportunities for nature-positive action.

Nature loss presents material financial risks across sectors, from supply chain disruptions to regulatory changes and reputational challenges.

## Key Topics

- **Ecosystem Services**: Understanding nature's contributions to business
- **TNFD Framework**: LEAP approach to nature-related disclosures
- **Nature Risk Assessment**: Identifying dependencies and impacts
- **Corporate Nature Strategies**: Setting science-based nature targets
- **Biodiversity Offsets**: Mitigation hierarchy and compensation
- **Deforestation-Free Supply Chains**: Commodity-driven forest loss
- **Species Protection**: Preventing biodiversity loss in operations

## Why It Matters

Over half of global GDP depends on nature. Companies face physical, transition, and liability risks from nature-related dependencies and impacts. The Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework sets targets that will shape regulatory expectations.
