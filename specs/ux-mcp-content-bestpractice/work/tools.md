
# ESG Tools & Resources

Curated directory of essential tools, databases, and platforms for ESG reporting, carbon accounting, data management, and sustainability implementation.

---

## Carbon Accounting & Climate Tools

### GHG Emissions Calculation

**[GHG Protocol Calculation Tools](https://ghgprotocol.org/ghg-emissions-calculation-tool)**  
Free Excel-based tools for calculating Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions across sectors.  
**Use For**: Corporate carbon footprinting, GHG inventory preparation  
**Cost**: Free

**[Watershed](https://watershed.com/)**  
Carbon accounting and climate program management platform.  
**Features**: Automated data collection, Scope 3 estimation, decarbonization roadmap, supplier engagement  
**Use For**: Enterprise carbon management  
**Cost**: Enterprise pricing (contact for quote)

**[Persefoni](https://persefoni.com/)**  
Carbon accounting and climate disclosure platform aligned with TCFD, IFRS S2, SEC.  
**Features**: Carbon accounting, scenario analysis, regulatory reporting, assurance-ready data  
**Use For**: Financial institutions, corporates preparing climate disclosures  
**Cost**: Enterprise pricing

**[Plan A](https://plana.earth/)**  
Carbon management and ESG reporting platform for SMEs and enterprises.  
**Features**: Carbon footprint calculation, reduction planning, ESG reporting  
**Use For**: Mid-market companies  
**Cost**: Subscription-based

---

### Emission Factor Databases

**[DEFRA Emission Factors](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/government-conversion-factors-for-company-reporting)**  
UK government emission factors for Scope 1, 2, 3 calculations.  
**Coverage**: Energy, transport, waste, water, materials  
**Cost**: Free

**[EPA Emission Factors](https://www.epa.gov/climateleadership/ghg-emission-factors-hub)**  
US EPA emission factors for GHG inventory.  
**Coverage**: Stationary combustion, mobile sources, electricity  
**Cost**: Free

**[Ecoinvent](https://ecoinvent.org/)**  
Life cycle inventory database with 18,000+ datasets.  
**Coverage**: Global, multi-sector, cradle-to-grave LCA data  
**Cost**: Subscription-based (academic discounts available)

**[EXIOBASE](https://www.exiobase.eu/)**  
Global multi-regional environmentally extended input-output (MRIO) database.  
**Use For**: Scope 3 spend-based calculations, economy-wide footprints  
**Cost**: Free (academic), paid (commercial)

---

### Climate Risk & Scenario Analysis

**[OS-Climate](https://os-climate.org/)**  
Open-source platform for climate risk analysis and scenario modeling.  
**Features**: Physical risk, transition risk, portfolio climate alignment  
**Cost**: Free (open-source)

**[Climate Risk Engines](https://www.climatex.com/)**  
Physical climate risk assessment for assets and portfolios.  
**Features**: Hazard mapping, asset-level risk scores, adaptation planning  
**Use For**: Real estate, infrastructure, supply chain risk  
**Cost**: Enterprise pricing

**[NGFS Scenarios](https://www.ngfs.net/ngfs-scenarios-portal/)**  
Network for Greening the Financial System climate scenarios.  
**Use For**: TCFD scenario analysis, stress testing  
**Cost**: Free

## ESG Reporting & Disclosure Platforms

### Comprehensive ESG Software

**[Workiva](https://www.workiva.com/solutions/esg-reporting)**  
Enterprise ESG reporting and data management platform.  
**Features**: Multi-framework reporting (GRI, SASB, TCFD, ESRS), data collection workflows, assurance-ready controls  
**Use For**: Large enterprises, multi-framework reporting  
**Cost**: Enterprise pricing

**[Enablon (Wolters Kluwer)](https://enablon.com/)**  
Integrated ESG, EHS, and risk management platform.  
**Features**: ESG reporting, carbon management, compliance, risk  
**Use For**: Enterprises with complex ESG/EHS needs  
**Cost**: Enterprise pricing

**[Sphera](https://sphera.com/)**  
ESG performance and risk management software.  
**Features**: ESG reporting, product stewardship, supply chain sustainability  
**Use For**: Manufacturing, chemicals, automotive sectors  
**Cost**: Enterprise pricing

**[Brightest](https://brightest.io/)**  
ESG data management and reporting platform.  
**Features**: Multi-framework reporting, stakeholder surveys, materiality assessment  
**Use For**: Mid-market companies  
**Cost**: Subscription-based

---

### Specialized Reporting Tools

**[CDP Online Response System](https://www.cdp.net/en/companies/companies-scores)**  
Platform for responding to CDP climate, water, and forests questionnaires.  
**Use For**: CDP disclosure, investor engagement  
**Cost**: Free for responders

**[GRI Data Partner Program](https://www.globalreporting.org/standards/)**  
Software partners certified for GRI Standards reporting.  
**Partners**: Workiva, Enablon, Brightest, and others  
**Use For**: GRI-aligned sustainability reporting

## Data & Benchmarking

### ESG Data Providers

**[MSCI ESG Ratings](https://www.msci.com/our-solutions/esg-investing/esg-ratings)**  
ESG ratings and research for 14,000+ companies.  
**Use For**: Benchmarking, investor analysis, peer comparison  
**Cost**: Subscription-based

**[Sustainalytics](https://www.sustainalytics.com/)**  
ESG risk ratings and research.  
**Use For**: ESG risk assessment, benchmarking  
**Cost**: Subscription-based

**[Refinitiv ESG Data](https://www.refinitiv.com/en/sustainable-finance/esg-scores)**  
ESG scores and data for 11,000+ companies.  
**Use For**: Financial analysis, portfolio screening  
**Cost**: Subscription-based

**[Bloomberg ESG Data](https://www.bloomberg.com/professional/dataset/esg/)**  
ESG data integrated into Bloomberg Terminal.  
**Use For**: Financial professionals, ESG analysis  
**Cost**: Bloomberg Terminal subscription

**[SBTi Target Dashboard](https://sciencebasedtargets.org/companies-taking-action)**  
Database of companies with science-based targets.

**[Corporate Knights Global 100](https://www.corporateknights.com/rankings/global-100-rankings/)**  
Annual ranking of most sustainable corporations.  
**Use For**: Benchmarking sustainability leaders  
**Cost**: Free (public rankings)

## Supply Chain & Supplier Management

**[EcoVadis](https://ecovadis.com/)**  
Supplier sustainability ratings and scorecards.  
**Features**: ESG assessments, corrective action plans, supplier engagement  
**Use For**: Supply chain due diligence, procurement  
**Cost**: Subscription-based

**[Manufacture 2030](https://manufacture2030.com/)**  
Supply chain carbon management and engagement platform.  
**Features**: Supplier carbon footprinting, reduction programs, data collection  
**Use For**: Scope 3 Category 1 (purchased goods/services)  
**Cost**: Subscription-based

**[Sedex](https://www.sedex.com/)**  
Ethical supply chain data exchange.  
**Features**: Labor practices, health & safety, environment, business ethics  
**Use For**: Social compliance, supply chain transparency  
**Cost**: Membership-based

## Nature & Biodiversity

**[TNFD LEAP Tool](https://tnfd.global/publication/getting-started-with-tnfd-leap-the-risk-and-opportunity-assessment-approach/)**  
Guidance for TNFD LEAP approach (Locate, Evaluate, Assess, Prepare).  
**Use For**: Nature-related risk assessment  
**Cost**: Free

**[IBAT (Integrated Biodiversity Assessment Tool)](https://www.ibat-alliance.org/)**  
Biodiversity data for business decision-making.  
**Features**: Protected areas, threatened species, key biodiversity areas  
**Use For**: Site-level biodiversity risk assessment  
**Cost**: Subscription-based

**[ENCORE (Exploring Natural Capital Opportunities, Risks and Exposure)](https://encorenature.org/)**  
Tool for understanding business dependencies and impacts on nature.  
**Features**: Sector-specific dependencies, impact drivers, materiality  
**Use For**: TNFD disclosures, nature risk assessment  
**Cost**: Free

**[Trase](https://trase.earth/)**  
Supply chain transparency for commodities linked to deforestation.  
**Coverage**: Soy, beef, palm oil, timber, coffee, cocoa  
**Use For**: Deforestation-free supply chains  
**Cost**: Free

Water risk mapping tool.  
**Features**: Water stress, flood risk, drought risk, regulatory risk  
**Use For**: Water risk assessment, facility siting

**[CEO Water Mandate Water Action Hub](https://wateractionhub.org/)**

**Features**: Water risk assessment, action planning, disclosure

**[Ellen MacArthur Foundation Circulytics](https://ellenmacarthurfoundation.org/resources/circulytics/overview)**  
Circular economy measurement tool.  
**Features**: Company-level circularity assessment  
**Use For**: Circular economy strategy  
**Cost**: Free

## Social & Human Rights

**[UN Guiding Principles Reporting Framework](https://www.ungpreporting.org/)**  
Framework and database for human rights reporting.  
**Features**: Reporting guidance, company examples  
**Use For**: Human rights due diligence disclosure  
**Cost**: Free

**[KnowTheChain](https://knowthechain.org/)**  
Benchmarks on forced labor in supply chains.  
**Coverage**: ICT, food & beverage, apparel & footwear  
**Use For**: Supply chain human rights assessment  
**Cost**: Free

**[Walk Free Global Slavery Index](https://www.walkfree.org/global-slavery-index/)**  
Data on modern slavery by country and sector.  
**Use For**: Supply chain risk assessment  
**Cost**: Free
