
# SDG Hub

**The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are 17 interconnected global goals adopted by all UN Member States in 2015 as part of the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development. They provide a shared blueprint for peace and prosperity for people and the planet, and serve as a critical framework for aligning business strategy with global sustainability priorities.**

The SDGs are increasingly integrated into ESG reporting frameworks, with GRI providing explicit SDG-to-disclosure mappings, the ISSB standards connecting to SDG-relevant sustainability topics, and the ESRS cross-referencing SDG targets. For businesses, the SDGs offer a common language for communicating sustainability impact to investors, customers, employees, and communities.
