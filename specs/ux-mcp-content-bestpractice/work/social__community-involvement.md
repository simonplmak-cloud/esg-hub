
# Community Involvement & Development

**Community involvement and development is a core subject of social responsibility as defined by ISO 26000, encompassing the proactive engagement of organisations with the communities in which they operate to contribute to sustainable development.**

This includes community participation, education and culture, employment creation and skills development, technology development and access, wealth and income creation, health, and social investment. Effective community engagement goes beyond philanthropy to create shared value — business strategies that generate economic value while addressing social needs. The social licence to operate depends on maintaining positive relationships with local communities and contributing to their development.
