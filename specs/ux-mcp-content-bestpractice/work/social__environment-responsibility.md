
# Environmental Responsibility (Social Perspective)

From the social pillar perspective, environmental responsibility concerns how organizations address environmental impacts that affect communities, workers, and society. This complements the environmental pillar by focusing on human dimensions including environmental justice, occupational health from environmental exposures, and community impacts of pollution.

---

## Key Social-Environmental Issues

### Environmental Justice
- **Disproportionate impacts**: Pollution burdens on marginalized communities
- **Access to environmental goods**: Clean air, water, green space
- **Participation in decisions**: Community voice in environmental permits and projects
- **Cumulative impacts**: Combined effects of multiple pollution sources

### Occupational Environmental Health
- **Chemical exposures**: Worker exposure to hazardous substances
- **Air quality**: Indoor air quality in workplaces
- **Noise and vibration**: Occupational exposure limits
- **Radiation**: Ionizing and non-ionizing radiation protection

### Community Environmental Impacts
- **Air pollution**: Emissions affecting nearby residents
- **Water pollution**: Discharge impacts on drinking water and livelihoods
- **Soil contamination**: Land degradation affecting agriculture
- **Noise and odor**: Nuisance impacts on quality of life

---

## Key Metrics

- **Community environmental complaints** (number)
- **Environmental justice assessments**: Operations in disadvantaged communities
- **Worker environmental health incidents**: Exposures exceeding limits
- **Remediation projects**: Contaminated site cleanups
- **Community health studies**: Epidemiological assessments
