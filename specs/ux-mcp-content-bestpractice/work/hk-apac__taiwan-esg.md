
# Taiwan ESG Requirements

Taiwan has introduced **mandatory sustainability reporting** for listed companies, with phased implementation based on company size and market capitalization.

## Regulatory Framework

**Financial Supervisory Commission (FSC)** — ESG disclosure regulations  
**Taiwan Stock Exchange (TWSE)** — Listing requirements  
**Environmental Protection Administration** — Environmental regulations

### Key Requirements

**Sustainability Reports** — Mandatory for companies meeting size/revenue thresholds  
**TCFD Alignment** — Climate-related disclosures following TCFD framework  
**GRI Standards** — Primary reporting framework  
**Third-Party Assurance** — Required for certain disclosures

## Implementation Timeline

**Phase 1 (2023):** Capital > NT$10 billion  
**Phase 2 (2024):** Capital > NT$5 billion  
**Phase 3 (2025):** All listed companies

## Why Taiwan ESG Matters

Taiwan is a **global semiconductor hub** (TSMC, MediaTek) and major electronics manufacturer. Taiwan's ESG regulations significantly impact global technology supply chains.
