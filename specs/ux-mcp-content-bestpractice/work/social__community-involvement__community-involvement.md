
# Community Involvement

**Community involvement refers to the proactive engagement of organisations with local communities to understand their needs, contribute to their development, and build mutually beneficial relationships.**

ISO 26000 identifies community involvement as both a core subject and a cross-cutting issue that supports all other areas of social responsibility. Effective community involvement includes stakeholder mapping and engagement to identify community needs and priorities, participatory approaches that empower communities in decision-making, transparent communication about the organisation's impacts and plans, grievance mechanisms accessible to community members, and long-term commitment to community development rather than short-term interventions. GRI 413 (Local Communities) requires disclosure of operations with significant actual and potential negative impacts on local communities, and the percentage of operations with implemented local community engagement, impact assessments, and development programmes.
