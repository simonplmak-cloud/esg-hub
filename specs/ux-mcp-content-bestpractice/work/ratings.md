
# ESG Ratings

**ESG ratings assess companies' environmental, social, and governance performance, providing scores and rankings used by investors for screening, integration, engagement, and benchmarking. The ESG ratings industry has grown rapidly but faces challenges around methodology transparency, rating divergence, and potential conflicts of interest.**
