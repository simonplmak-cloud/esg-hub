
# Risk Management

**Enterprise risk management (ERM) is the process by which organisations identify, assess, manage, and monitor risks that could affect the achievement of their objectives.**

The integration of ESG risks into ERM frameworks has become a governance imperative, driven by regulatory requirements, investor expectations, and the material financial impacts of sustainability-related risks. Climate-related risks (physical and transition), nature-related risks, human rights risks, and technology risks are increasingly recognised as enterprise-level risks requiring board oversight. The COSO ERM Framework and ISO 31000 provide widely used frameworks. TCFD/ISSB require disclosure of how climate risks are integrated into overall risk management. ESRS 2 IRO-1 requires disclosure of the process to identify and assess material sustainability-related impacts, risks, and opportunities.
