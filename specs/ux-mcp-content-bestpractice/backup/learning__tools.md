
# ESG Tools & Resources

**A directory of practical tools, data platforms, calculators, and software resources that support ESG measurement, reporting, analysis, and decision-making — from free open-source tools to professional platforms.**

Effective ESG management requires the right tools. This page catalogues the most useful resources for ESG practitioners, organised by function.

## Open-Source & Community Tools

The open-source community has developed several valuable ESG tools. [OS-Climate](https://os-climate.org/) is a Linux Foundation project building open-source tools for climate-aligned finance. [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) provides open data for ESG research and analysis. Carbon footprint calculator tools are available from multiple providers including the [GHG Protocol](https://ghgprotocol.org/calculation-tools-and-guidance) for organisational use and [CoolClimate](https://coolclimate.berkeley.edu/) for individual use.

---

## Selecting the Right Tools

When choosing ESG tools, consider your organisation's size, reporting obligations, and maturity level. Small organisations may start with free calculators and templates, while larger companies may need integrated ESG management platforms. Key selection criteria include framework alignment (does the tool support your required standards?), data integration capabilities, audit trail functionality, and scalability.
