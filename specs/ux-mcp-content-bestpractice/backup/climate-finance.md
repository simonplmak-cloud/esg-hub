# Climate Finance Hub

Climate finance encompasses the financial flows, instruments, and mechanisms that support climate mitigation and adaptation. This section covers carbon markets, green bonds, climate risk assessment, and transition finance.

Understanding climate finance is essential for companies seeking to align with net-zero commitments, access green capital, and manage climate-related financial risks.

## Key Topics

- **Carbon Markets**: Voluntary and compliance carbon markets
- **Carbon Credits & Offsets**: Quality standards and verification
- **Green Bonds**: Sustainable debt instruments
- **Climate Risk Assessment**: Physical and transition risks
- **Net-Zero Commitments**: Science-based targets and pathways
- **Science Based Targets (SBTi)**: Corporate emission reduction goals
- **Transition Finance**: Financing the low-carbon transition

## Why It Matters

Climate change presents systemic financial risks. Companies that proactively manage climate exposure and access green finance will be better positioned for the transition to a low-carbon economy.
