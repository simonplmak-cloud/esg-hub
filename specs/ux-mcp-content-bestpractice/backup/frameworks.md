
# ESG Frameworks

**ESG frameworks provide the structures, principles, and methodologies through which organisations identify, measure, manage, and report their environmental, social, and governance performance. The framework landscape has consolidated significantly, with the ISSB, ESRS, and GRI emerging as the primary global standards.**

Understanding the relationships between frameworks — their scope, materiality approach, and intended audience — is essential for effective ESG reporting and strategy. This section provides an overview of the major frameworks and their interconnections.

---

## Framework Landscape

| Framework | Scope | Materiality | Primary Audience |
|-----------|-------|-------------|-----------------|
| [ISSB (IFRS S1/S2)](/standards/ifrs-s1-s2/) | Global baseline | Financial materiality | Investors |
| [ESRS](/standards/esrs/) | EU mandatory | Double materiality | All stakeholders |
| [GRI](/standards/gri/) | Global voluntary | Impact materiality | All stakeholders |
| [TCFD](/standards/tcfd/) | Climate-focused | Financial materiality | Investors |
| [TNFD](/standards/tnfd/) | Nature-focused | Financial materiality | Investors |
| [GHG Protocol](/standards/climate-nature/ghg-protocol/) | Emissions | N/A (measurement) | All |
| [SBTi](/standards/climate-nature/sbti/) | Climate targets | N/A (target-setting) | All |
