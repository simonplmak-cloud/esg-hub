
# ESG Video Library

**A curated collection of educational videos, webinars, conference presentations, and expert interviews covering the full spectrum of ESG topics — from introductory overviews to advanced technical discussions.**

Video content offers an accessible way to learn about complex ESG topics, hear directly from standard-setters and practitioners, and stay current with rapidly evolving developments. This library organises the best freely available ESG video content by topic.

---

## Reporting Standards & Frameworks

**ISSB & IFRS Sustainability Standards**
The [IFRS Foundation YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@IFRSFoundation) provides official explanations of IFRS S1 and S2, including board meeting recordings, educational webinars, and implementation guidance sessions. Key videos include the ISSB Chair's overview of the standards and technical staff explanations of specific requirements. The [ISSB Standards Navigator](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/ifrs-sustainability-standards-navigator/) provides the full text of the standards for reference.

**GRI Standards**
[GRI's YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@GlobalReportingInitiative) hosts webinar series covering standard updates, sector-specific guidance, and implementation case studies. The [GRI Academy](https://www.globalreporting.org/public-policy-partnerships/the-gri-academy/) also offers introductory video courses on the GRI Standards framework.

**ESRS & CSRD**
[EFRAG](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCxcljiiUM2JD02SMuFUTUwA) publishes explanatory videos on each ESRS standard, implementation guidance, and Q&A sessions. The [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/business-economy-euro/doing-business-eu/corporate-sustainability-due-diligence_en) also provides overview videos on the CSRD and its requirements.

**TCFD & Climate Disclosure**
Note: The TCFD Knowledge Hub closed on 31 December 2025. Climate disclosure guidance has been consolidated under the [ISSB (IFRS S2)](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/ifrs-sustainability-standards-navigator/ifrs-s2-climate-related-disclosures/). Historical TCFD resources remain available through the [FSB archive](https://www.fsb-tcfd.org/).
**[Sustainability Explainer Video Series – Chapters 1–8](https://bursasustain.bursamalaysia.com/droplet-details/resources/sustainability-explainer-video-series)** — 8-chapter official video series by Bursa Malaysia providing practical guidance. *Bursa Malaysia, Self-paced.*
**[Post-ISSB ESG Disclosure Framework Landscape (Japanese)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lp70P3LU61U)** — JPX seminar on the ESG disclosure framework landscape after ISSB. *Japan Exchange Group (JPX / Tokyo Stock Exchange), 30 minutes.*
**[JPX ESG Knowledge Hub – Practical Seminars for ESG Disclosure (Steps 1–5) (Japanese)](https://www.jpx.co.jp/corporate/sustainability/esgknowledgehub/esg-seminar/index.html)** — Structured 5-step video seminar series on the JPX ESG Knowledge Hub. *Japan Exchange Group (JPX / Tokyo Stock Exchange), Self-paced.*

## Climate & Environment

**Climate Science Fundamentals**
The [IPCC](https://www.ipcc.ch/) communication materials include video summaries of its Assessment Reports, providing authoritative overviews of climate science, impacts, and mitigation pathways. [Project Drawdown](https://drawdown.org/) offers engaging videos on climate solutions.

**Nature & Biodiversity**
[TNFD's YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCxr65yI_szV8UODfmyuhTzw) explains the LEAP approach to nature-related risk assessment. The [World Economic Forum](https://www.youtube.com/@wef) nature-related content covers biodiversity loss, ecosystem services, and natural capital accounting. See also the [TNFD Recommendations](https://tnfd.global/recommendations-of-the-tnfd/).

**Circular Economy**
The [Ellen MacArthur Foundation YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@EllenMacArthurFoundation) is the definitive resource for circular economy concepts, with case studies, animations, and expert interviews. Visit [ellenmacarthurfoundation.org](https://www.ellenmacarthurfoundation.org/) for full resources.
**[TNFD 101: Introduction to the Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures](https://tnfd.global/webinar/tnfd-101-introduction-to-the-taskforce-on-nature-related-financial-disclosures-tnfd/)** — Introductory webinar explaining why the TNFD was created, its objectives. *Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD), 1 hour.*

## Investment & Finance

**ESG Integration**
[CFA Institute's YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@CFAinstitute) hosts ESG-related webinars covering integration techniques, stewardship, and the evolving regulatory landscape. [PRI's](https://www.unpri.org/) annual conference recordings provide insights from leading institutional investors.

**Sustainable Finance**
The [Network for Greening the Financial System (NGFS)](https://www.ngfs.net/en) publishes webinars on climate scenarios, green finance, and central bank approaches to climate risk. The [ICMA Green Bond Principles](https://www.icmagroup.org/sustainable-finance/the-principles-guidelines-and-handbooks/green-bond-principles-gbp/) webinar series covers sustainable debt markets.
**[2024 HKGFA Annual Forum Session 1 – Transition Plans in Practice: Credible Pathways](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zjnhtkc_ZYE)** — Panel session from the 2024 HKGFA Annual Forum 'Financing Asia's Net Zero. *Hong Kong Green Finance Association (HKGFA), 1.5 hours.*
**[HKGFA Transition Training Series: Sectoral Webinar on Green Finance and the Data Center Industry](https://www.hkgreenfinance.org/hkgfa-transition-training-series-sectoral-webinar-on-green-finance-and-the-data-center-industry/)** — Sectoral webinar exploring green finance applications and sustainability. *Hong Kong Green Finance Association (HKGFA), 1.5 hours.*
**[HKGFA Transition Planning Webinar Series #3 – Disclosures and Standards: Transition Considerations for Asset Managers](https://youtu.be/iEerXmns11M)** — Session 3 of the HKGFA Transition Planning Webinar Series exploring disclosures. *Hong Kong Green Finance Association (HKGFA), 1.5 hours.*
**[GPIF Engagement Effectiveness Verification Project (Japanese)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Z88jOUxzOlA)** — GPIF project verifying the effectiveness of engagement activities. *Japan Exchange Group (JPX / Tokyo Stock Exchange), 30 minutes.*
**[CBIN Webinar Series: financial sustainability for business incubators and accelerators](https://www.worldbank.org/en/olc/course/58367)** — Webinar on accelerator and incubator funding profiles and sustainability. *World Bank Group, Self-paced.*
