
# ESG Rating Agencies in Governance

**ESG rating agencies assess companies' environmental, social, and governance performance, providing scores and rankings used by investors for screening, integration, and engagement.**

Major providers include MSCI ESG, Sustainalytics (Morningstar), S&P Global, ISS ESG, and CDP. Governance factors typically account for a significant portion of overall ESG scores, covering board structure, executive compensation, shareholder rights, business ethics, and transparency. However, ESG ratings face criticism for low correlation between providers, lack of transparency in methodologies, and potential conflicts of interest. The EU and other jurisdictions are developing regulatory frameworks for ESG rating providers to improve transparency and reliability.
