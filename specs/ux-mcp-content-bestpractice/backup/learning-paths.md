
# ESG Learning Paths

Structured learning journeys to build ESG expertise, from foundational concepts to advanced implementation. Each path combines books, standards documentation, and practical guides.

---

## Path 1: ESG Reporting Fundamentals

**For**: Professionals new to ESG reporting  
**Duration**: 4-6 weeks  
**Outcome**: Understand major ESG frameworks and prepare basic sustainability disclosures

### Week 1-2: Foundation
1. Read [About ESG](/about/) - Understand ESG pillars and why it matters
2. Study [Materiality Assessment](/practice/materiality-assessment/) - Learn to identify material topics
3. Review [Glossary](/glossary/) - Familiarize with ESG terminology
4. Read **ESG Reporting Made Simple (IFRS/SASB)** (Chapters 1-3) - IFRS S1 fundamentals

### Week 3-4: Frameworks
5. Study [IFRS S1 & S2](/standards/ifrs-s1-s2/) - Investor-focused sustainability disclosures
6. Study [GRI Standards](/standards/gri/) - Impact-focused reporting
7. Study [TCFD](/standards/tcfd/) - Climate-related financial disclosures
8. Read **ESG & GRI Reporting Made Simple** (Chapters 1-4) - GRI fundamentals

### Week 5-6: Practice
9. Complete [Stakeholder Engagement](/practice/stakeholder-engagement/) guide
10. Review [Data Management](/practice/data-management/) best practices
11. Study reporting examples from [Case Studies](/case-studies/)
12. **Exercise**: Draft materiality matrix for your organization

**Next Steps**: Proceed to Path 2 (Climate Reporting) or Path 3 (EU CSRD)

## Path 2: Climate Reporting & Net-Zero Strategy

**For**: Professionals focused on climate disclosures and decarbonization  
**Duration**: 6-8 weeks  
**Outcome**: Prepare TCFD/IFRS S2 disclosures and set science-based targets

### Week 1-2: Climate Fundamentals
1. Study [Climate Change](/environmental/climate-change/) - Planetary boundaries and climate science
2. Read **TCFD Reporting Made Simple** (Full book) - TCFD framework
3. Study [TCFD Standard](/standards/tcfd/) - Detailed guidance
4. Study [IFRS S2](/standards/ifrs-s1-s2/) - Climate-related disclosures

### Week 3-4: GHG Accounting
5. Study [GHG Protocol](/standards/ghg-protocol/) - Scope 1/2/3 methodology
6. Read **Carbon Accounting in Practice** (Chapters 1-5) - Technical calculations
7. Study [Data Management](/practice/data-management/) - Scope 3 data collection
8. **Exercise**: Calculate your organization's carbon footprint

### Week 5-6: Science-Based Targets
9. Study [SBTi](/standards/sbti/) - Science-based target validation
10. Read **Science-Based Targets: A Practical Guide** (Full book)
11. Study [Target Setting](/practice/target-setting/) - Implementation guidance
12. **Exercise**: Draft 1.5°C-aligned targets for your organization

### Week 7-8: Advanced Topics
13. Study climate scenario analysis (TCFD guidance)
14. Review [Climate Risk Quantification in Practice](/books/) - Financial impact modeling
15. Study [Assurance & Verification](/practice/assurance-verification/) - GHG assurance
16. **Exercise**: Prepare TCFD disclosure draft

**Next Steps**: Implement decarbonization roadmap, pursue SBTi validation

## Path 3: EU CSRD Compliance

**For**: Companies subject to EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive  
**Duration**: 8-10 weeks  
**Outcome**: Prepare for CSRD reporting and ESRS compliance

### Week 1-2: CSRD Overview
1. Study [EU Regulations](/regulations/european-union/) - CSRD scope and timeline
2. Read **ESG Reporting Made Simple (EU CSRD/ESRS)** (Chapters 1-3) - CSRD fundamentals
3. Study [ESRS](/standards/esrs/) - European Sustainability Reporting Standards
4. Review CSRD implementation timeline for your company size

### Week 3-4: Double Materiality
5. Study [Materiality Assessment](/practice/materiality-assessment/) - Double materiality process
6. Read ESRS 2 SBM-3 guidance on material impacts, risks, opportunities
7. Study [Stakeholder Engagement](/practice/stakeholder-engagement/) - ESRS requirements
8. **Exercise**: Conduct double materiality assessment

### Week 5-6: Environmental Standards (ESRS E1-E5)
9. Study [Climate Change](/environmental/climate-change/) + ESRS E1
10. Study [Biodiversity](/environmental/biodiversity/) + ESRS E4
11. Study [Water](/environmental/water/) + ESRS E3
12. Study [Circular Economy](/environmental/) + ESRS E5

### Week 7-8: Social & Governance Standards
13. Study [Social Pillar](/social/) + ESRS S1-S4
14. Study [Governance Pillar](/governance/) + ESRS G1
15. Study [Human Rights](/social/human-rights/) - ESRS S1 own workforce

### Week 9-10: Implementation
16. Study [Data Management](/practice/data-management/) - ESRS data requirements
17. Study [Assurance & Verification](/practice/assurance-verification/) - CSRD assurance
18. Review digital tagging requirements (XBRL/iXBRL)
19. **Exercise**: Draft ESRS 2 General Disclosures

**Next Steps**: Implement ESRS data collection, engage assurance provider

## Path 4: Nature & Biodiversity Reporting

**For**: Companies in nature-dependent sectors (agriculture, forestry, mining, real estate)  
**Duration**: 4-6 weeks  
**Outcome**: Prepare TNFD disclosures and understand nature-related risks

### Week 1-2: Nature Fundamentals
1. Study [Biodiversity](/environmental/biodiversity/) - Planetary boundaries
2. Study [Land Use Change](/environmental/land-use-change/) - Deforestation and habitat loss
3. Read **TNFD Made Simple** (Full book) - TNFD framework
4. Study [TNFD Standard](/standards/tnfd/) - Detailed guidance

### Week 3-4: LEAP Approach
5. Study TNFD LEAP: Locate (identify interface with nature)
6. Study TNFD LEAP: Evaluate (dependencies and impacts)
7. Study TNFD LEAP: Assess (material risks and opportunities)
8. Study TNFD LEAP: Prepare (disclosure and action)
9. **Exercise**: Complete LEAP assessment for your organization

### Week 5-6: Advanced Topics
10. Study [SBTN](/standards/sbtn/) - Science-based targets for nature
11. Read **Biodiversity and Ecosystem Services Accounting** - Technical metrics
12. Study integration with TCFD/IFRS S2 (climate-nature nexus)
13. **Exercise**: Draft TNFD disclosure

**Next Steps**: Set science-based targets for nature (SBTN), integrate into business strategy

## Path 5: Social Responsibility & Human Rights

**For**: Companies with significant labor or supply chain risks  
**Duration**: 4-6 weeks  
**Outcome**: Implement human rights due diligence and prepare social disclosures

### Week 1-2: Human Rights Fundamentals
1. Study [Human Rights](/social/human-rights/) - UN Guiding Principles
2. Study [Labor Practices](/social/labor-practices/) - ILO conventions
3. Read **Social Responsibility & Human Rights Reporting** (Chapters 1-4)
4. Study GRI 400 series (social topics)

### Week 3-4: Due Diligence
5. Study human rights due diligence (HRDD) process
6. Study salient human rights issues identification
7. Study [Fair Operating Practices](/social/fair-operating-practices/) - Supply chain
8. **Exercise**: Conduct salient human rights assessment

### Week 5-6: Implementation
9. Study [Stakeholder Engagement](/practice/stakeholder-engagement/) - Rights holders
10. Study grievance mechanisms and remedy
11. Review mandatory HRDD laws (EU CSDDD, German Supply Chain Act)
12. **Exercise**: Draft human rights policy and HRDD process

**Next Steps**: Implement HRDD, engage with suppliers, establish grievance mechanism

---

## Path 6: ESG Data & Assurance

**For**: ESG data managers, sustainability analysts, assurance preparers  
**Duration**: 4-6 weeks  
**Outcome**: Build robust ESG data systems and prepare for external assurance

### Week 1-2: Data Management
1. Study [Data Management](/practice/data-management/) - ESG data systems
2. Read **ESG Data Management & Assurance** (Chapters 1-3)
3. Study GHG Protocol data quality requirements
4. Study Scope 3 data collection methods

### Week 3-4: Technical Accounting
5. Read **Carbon Accounting in Practice** - GHG calculations
6. Read **Water Accounting in Practice** - Water footprint
7. Read **Social Impact Accounting** - SROI methodology
8. **Exercise**: Implement data collection system for material metrics

### Week 5-6: Assurance
9. Study [Assurance & Verification](/practice/assurance-verification/) - ISAE 3000/3410
10. Study internal controls for ESG data
11. Review CSRD assurance requirements
12. **Exercise**: Prepare for limited assurance engagement

**Next Steps**: Engage assurance provider, implement controls, pursue reasonable assurance

---

## Path 7: ESG Strategy & Integration

**For**: Executives, board members, strategy teams  
**Duration**: 4-6 weeks  
**Outcome**: Embed ESG into corporate strategy and governance

### Week 1-2: Strategic Foundation
1. Study [ESG Integration](/practice/esg-integration/) - Maturity model
2. Read **ESG Strategy & Integration** (Full book)
3. Study [Board Structure](/governance/board-structure/) - Board oversight
4. Study [Executive Compensation](/governance/executive-compensation/) - ESG-linked pay

### Week 3-4: Risk & Opportunity
5. Study [Risk Management](/governance/risk-management/) - ESG risks in ERM
6. Study [Materiality Assessment](/practice/materiality-assessment/) - Strategic priorities
7. Review ESG risks and opportunities by sector
8. **Exercise**: Integrate ESG into strategic planning process

### Week 5-6: Implementation
9. Study [Target Setting](/practice/target-setting/) - Ambitious ESG targets
10. Study sustainability-driven innovation
11. Study ESG communication and investor engagement
12. **Exercise**: Develop ESG strategy aligned with business strategy

**Next Steps**: Implement ESG strategy, establish board oversight, link executive compensation to ESG

## Customizing Your Learning Path

**For Beginners**: Start with Path 1 (ESG Reporting Fundamentals)

**For Climate-Focused Roles**: Path 2 (Climate Reporting & Net-Zero)

**For EU Companies**: Path 3 (EU CSRD Compliance)

**For Nature-Dependent Sectors**: Path 4 (Nature & Biodiversity)

**For Supply Chain/HR Roles**: Path 5 (Social Responsibility & Human Rights)

**For Data/Analytics Roles**: Path 6 (ESG Data & Assurance)

**For Leadership/Strategy**: Path 7 (ESG Strategy & Integration)

**For Comprehensive Mastery**: Complete Paths 1 → 2 → 3 → 7 (16-24 weeks)
