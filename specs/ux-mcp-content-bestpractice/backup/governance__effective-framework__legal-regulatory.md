
# Legal & Regulatory Framework

**The legal and regulatory framework for corporate governance encompasses company law, securities regulation, listing rules, and governance codes that together define the rights and obligations of companies, directors, shareholders, and stakeholders.**

Effective frameworks balance mandatory requirements with voluntary best practices, providing clear rules while allowing flexibility for different company circumstances. Key elements include company formation and registration requirements, director duties and liabilities, shareholder rights and protections, disclosure and transparency requirements, and enforcement mechanisms. The interaction between hard law (mandatory legislation) and soft law (governance codes operating on a comply-or-explain basis) varies by jurisdiction but both play essential roles.
