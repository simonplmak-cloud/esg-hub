
# Consumer Issues

**Consumer issues encompass the responsibilities organisations have toward their customers and consumers, including product safety, fair marketing, data protection, access to essential services, and sustainable consumption.**

ISO 26000 identifies consumer issues as a core subject of social responsibility.
