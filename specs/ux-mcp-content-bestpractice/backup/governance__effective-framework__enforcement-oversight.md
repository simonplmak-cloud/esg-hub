
# Enforcement & Oversight

**Effective enforcement is essential for corporate governance frameworks to function properly.**

Without credible enforcement mechanisms, even well-designed rules and standards will not achieve their intended outcomes. Enforcement encompasses securities regulation and market supervision, corporate law enforcement through courts, stock exchange listing rule compliance monitoring, and governance code compliance on a comply-or-explain basis. Regulators such as the SEC (US), FCA (UK), ESMA (EU), and SFC (Hong Kong) play critical roles in market oversight, investigating misconduct, and imposing sanctions. The effectiveness of enforcement varies significantly across jurisdictions and is a key factor in investor confidence and market development.
